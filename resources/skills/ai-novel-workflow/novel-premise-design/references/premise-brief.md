# Premise Brief

## Minimum Viable Premise

- `target_reader`
- `platform_fit`
- `genre_lane`
- `reader_promise`
- `protagonist_desire`
- `protagonist_pressure`
- `core_conflict_engine`
- `novelty_hook`
- `golden_opening_question`
- `first_volume_direction`
- `risks`
- `avoid_rules`

## Viability Checks

- Can the premise produce 20 chapter conflicts without changing genre?
- Is the reader reward visible before detailed worldbuilding?
- Does the protagonist have agency in the first major choice?
- Is the hook easy to explain in one sentence?
