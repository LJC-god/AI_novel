# Outline Node Rules

## A Writable Node Must Include

- A concrete scene or sequence, not an abstract theme.
- A conflict that can be dramatized.
- A protagonist action or decision.
- A reader reward or new question.
- A link to previous cause and next pressure.

## Avoid

- Pure exposition chapters.
- Repeated argument beats with no status change.
- New powers or clues without cost.
- Chapter endings that only say "more will happen".

## Batch Rhythm

Across 5-10 nodes, alternate pressure types:

- external obstacle
- relationship tension
- internal flaw
- mystery reveal
- payoff
- reversal
