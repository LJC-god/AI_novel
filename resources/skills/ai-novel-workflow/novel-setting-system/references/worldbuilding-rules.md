# Worldbuilding Rules

## Consistency Checks

- Every power has cost, limit, detection, countermeasure, and social consequence.
- Every faction has resource, ideology, public face, private agenda, and pressure point.
- Every important location has narrative function: refuge, threat, market, prison, stage, archive, or battlefield.
- Avoid adding lore that cannot create a scene, conflict, clue, or choice.
- Record exceptions as named exceptions with a cost and owner.

## Entry Shape

Use this compact shape when possible:

- `definition`
- `rule`
- `cost`
- `limits`
- `story_use`
- `risk`
