# Market Scan Checklist

## Signals To Capture

- Platform: target platform and whether it favors free, paid, short, long, female, male, new-media, or serial formats.
- Reader hook: the first emotional promise visible from title, synopsis, opening conflict, and tags.
- Conflict engine: revenge, face-slapping, romance tension, survival pressure, mystery, upgrade, status reversal, or family/workplace pressure.
- Differentiation: one concrete twist that changes the familiar template without breaking the reader promise.
- Saturation: count how often the same premise pattern appears in the sample.
- Execution burden: worldbuilding load, update pressure, research cost, compliance risk, and style difficulty.

## Scoring

Rate each candidate from 1 to 5:

- Demand: visible reader interest or platform fit.
- Freshness: avoids direct copycat feeling.
- Execution: can be written consistently for the target length.
- Hook strength: can be explained in one sentence.
- Expansion: enough conflicts for the requested chapter count.

Recommend only ideas with total score 18 or higher unless the user explicitly wants experimental work.
