# Cover Prompt Fields

## Prompt Shape

- `platform`
- `genre_signal`
- `main_subject`
- `pose_or_expression`
- `background`
- `color_palette`
- `lighting`
- `style`
- `title_space`
- `negative_prompt`

## Practical Rules

- Do not crowd the cover with many characters.
- Keep the title area clean.
- Make the genre readable at thumbnail size.
- Avoid vague cinematic words without concrete subject details.
