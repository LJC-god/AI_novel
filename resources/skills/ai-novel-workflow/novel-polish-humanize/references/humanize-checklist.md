# Humanize Checklist

## Remove Or Rewrite

- Meta phrases: "接下来", "值得注意的是", "毫无疑问", "从某种意义上".
- Over-summary: paragraphs that explain what the reader should feel.
- Uniform sentence chains: repeated "他/她 + verb" starts.
- Empty intensity: "极其", "无比", "深深地" when no image supports it.
- Essay logic: "首先/其次/最后", "一方面/另一方面".

## Add

- Concrete gestures.
- Interruptions.
- Character-specific word choices.
- Uneven breath and sentence length.
- Small physical details tied to emotion.
