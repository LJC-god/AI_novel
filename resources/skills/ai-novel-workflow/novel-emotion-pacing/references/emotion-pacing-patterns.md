# Emotion Pacing Patterns

## Common Curves

- Suppress then release: humiliation, pressure, proof, reversal, public payoff.
- Suspense ladder: question, partial clue, wrong answer, cost, clearer question.
- Romance tension: desire, interruption, misread, exposed care, retreat.
- Upgrade rhythm: limit, attempt, failure, insight, costed breakthrough.
- Healing curve: wound, denial, small trust, relapse, chosen vulnerability.

## Density Rules

- Short fiction needs visible emotional movement every 500-1000 words.
- Long serial chapters need at least one reader reward or sharper question per chapter.
- Avoid stacking the same reward type three times in a row.
